# Event-Based MSCKF (EVIO) Implementation Guide

This document provides a step-by-step developer guide for implementing an **Event-based Visual Inertial Odometry (EVIO)** pipeline using a **Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter (MSCKF)** backend.

The architecture combines two core foundational papers:

1. **Frontend (Event Tracking):** *Event-based Visual Inertial Odometry* (Zhu, Atanasov, & Daniilidis, CVPR 2017) — Asynchronous feature tracking in spatiotemporal event windows using Expectation-Maximization (EM).
2. **Backend (MSCKF State Estimation):** *A Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter for Vision-aided Inertial Navigation* (Mourikis & Roumeliotis, ICRA 2007) — An EKF with a structureless measurement model that marginalizes 3D feature coordinates out of the state vector, maintaining $O(L)$ linear complexity in the number of features.

---

## System Architecture & State Definition

Before executing the pipeline steps, the estimator must maintain a state vector $X_k$ containing the current evolving IMU state $X_{IMU}$ and a sliding window of $N$ past camera poses (up to a maximum of $N_{max}$ poses).

### State Vector Representation

#### 1. Evolving IMU State $(16 \times 1)$

$$
X_{IMU} = \begin{bmatrix} {}^I_G\bar{q}^T & b_g^T & {}^G v_I^T & b_a^T & {}^G p_I^T \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

Here, ${}^I_G\bar{q}$ is the unit quaternion rotating from the global frame $\{G\}$ to the IMU frame $\{I\}$, $b_g, b_a \in \mathbb{R}^3$ are the gyroscope and accelerometer biases, and ${}^G v_I, {}^G p_I \in \mathbb{R}^3$ are the global IMU velocity and position.

#### 2. IMU Error-State $(15 \times 1)$

$$
\tilde{X}_{IMU} = \begin{bmatrix} \delta\theta_I^T & \tilde{b}_g^T & {}^G\tilde{v}_I^T & \tilde{b}_a^T & {}^G\tilde{p}_I^T \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

Standard additive error $(\tilde{x} = x - \hat{x})$ is used for position, velocity, and biases, while a minimal 3-DoF small-angle orientation error $\delta\theta$ is used for quaternions via $\bar{q} = \delta\bar{q} \otimes \hat{\bar{q}}$ with:

$$
\delta\bar{q} \simeq \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{2}\delta\theta^T & 1 \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

#### 3. Full Augmented EKF State Vector & Error-State $((15 + 6N) \times 1)$

$$
\hat{X}_k = \begin{bmatrix} \hat{X}_{IMU_k}^T & {}^{C_1}_G\hat{\bar{q}}^T & {}^G\hat{p}_{C_1}^T & \cdots & {}^{C_N}_G\hat{\bar{q}}^T & {}^G\hat{p}_{C_N}^T \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

$$
\tilde{X}_k = \begin{bmatrix} \tilde{X}_{IMU_k}^T & \delta\theta_{C_1}^T & {}^G\tilde{p}_{C_1}^T & \cdots & \delta\theta_{C_N}^T & {}^G\tilde{p}_{C_N}^T \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

#### 4. Partitioned Covariance Matrix $((15 + 6N) \times (15 + 6N))$

$$
P_{k\mid k} = \begin{bmatrix} P_{II_{k\mid k}} & P_{IC_{k\mid k}} \\ P_{IC_{k\mid k}}^T & P_{CC_{k\mid k}} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Here, $P_{II_{k\mid k}}$ is $15 \times 15$, $P_{CC_{k\mid k}}$ is $6N \times 6N$, and $P_{IC_{k\mid k}}$ is $15 \times 6N$.

## Step 1: Spatiotemporal Optical Flow Estimation (Frontend - EM Step 1)

### General Overview

Because event cameras output asynchronous pixel-level brightness changes rather than intensity frames, traditional optical flow (like KLT) cannot be directly applied. Within a small adaptive time window $[T_i, T_{i+1}]$ where $dt_i = T_{i+1} - T_i$, events generated by a single 3D landmark form a straight line in spacetime whose slope is the optical flow $u$. This step uses an Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm to softly associate incoming events with forward-propagated template events from the previous window and solve for the linear optical flow $u$ of each tracked feature.

### Inputs

* **Event Stream:** $\mathcal{E} = \{(x_k, t_k)\}$ for $t_k \in [T_i, T_{i+1}]$, where $x_k \in \mathbb{R}^2$ is in normalized pixel coordinates.
* **Tracked Features:** Current 2D feature centers $f(T_i)$ at the start of the window.
* **Prior Feature Template:** $\tilde{l}_j^{i-1}$, the forward-propagated events from the previous window $W_{i-1}$.
* **Window Size Parameters:** Spatial neighborhood radius $\xi$ (e.g., $31 \times 31$ window) and temporal duration $dt_i$.
* **Measurement Noise Covariance:** $\Sigma = 2I_2$.

### Outputs

* **Estimated Optical Flow:** $u_i \in \mathbb{R}^2$ for each feature in the window $[T_i, T_{i+1}]$.
* **Motion-Corrected Event Template:** $\tilde{l}_k^i$ (events propagated to $T_{i+1}$ using $u_i$).

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Collect Spatiotemporal Window Events $W_i$

Define relative time $\bar{t}_k = t_k - T_i$. Select events around feature $f(T_i)$:

$$
W_i = \left\{ (x_k, t_k) \in \mathcal{E} \;\middle\mid\; \parallel (x_k - \bar{t}_k u) - f(T_i) \parallel \le \xi, \; t_k \in [T_i, T_{i+1}] \right\}
$$

#### 2. Compute Prior Template (from previous step $i-1$)

$$
\tilde{l}_j^{i-1} = \left\{ x + (T_i - t)u_{i-1} \;\middle\mid\; (x, t) \in W_{i-1} \right\}
$$

*Note: Subsample $\tilde{l}_j^{i-1}$ using sphere decimation with a $1\text{ px}$ radius and store in a k-d tree for fast Mahalanobis distance lookup.*

#### 3. Iterate EM Step 1 Until Convergence $(\text{cost} \le \epsilon_1)$

**E-Step (Soft Data Association):** Compute association weights $r_{kj}$ between current event $k \in \{1, \dots, n_i\}$ and prior template point $j \in \{1, \dots, n_{i-1}\}$ using a Gaussian PDF $\phi(\cdot; \mu, \Sigma)$ (rejecting pairs with Mahalanobis distance $> 4\text{ px}$):

$$
r_{kj} = \frac{\phi(x_k - \bar{t}_k u; \tilde{l}_j^{i-1}, \Sigma)}{\sum_{j'=1}^{n_{i-1}} \phi(x_k - \bar{t}_k u; \tilde{l}_{j'}^{i-1}, \Sigma)}
$$

**M-Step (Weighted Least Squares Flow Update):**

$$
u = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i-1}} r_{kj} (x_k - \tilde{l}_j^{i-1})\bar{t}_k}{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i-1}} r_{kj} \bar{t}_k^2}
$$

**Special Case (Feature Initialization):** When a feature is first detected and no prior template $\tilde{l}_j^{i-1}$ exists, substitute the template with current back-propagated events $(x_j - \bar{t}_j u)$ and use:

$$
u = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} r_{kj} (x_k - x_j)(\bar{t}_k - \bar{t}_j)}{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} r_{kj} (\bar{t}_k - \bar{t}_j)^2}
$$

**Cost Function Check:**

$$
\text{cost} = \sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i-1}} r_{kj} \parallel (x_k - \bar{t}_k u) - \tilde{l}_j^{i-1} \parallel^2
$$

## Step 2: Affine Template Alignment & Outlier Rejection (Frontend - EM Step 2)

### General Overview

Integrating optical flow over multiple windows causes feature positions to drift. To eliminate drift, the motion-corrected events from Step 1 are warped back to the initial camera pose $T_{i^*}$ where the feature was first observed. Using the 3D relative rotation ${}^{i^*}R_i$ from the MSCKF state, a second EM loop estimates a 2D scaling $\sigma$ and translation correction $b$ against the original onset template $\tilde{l}_j^{i^*}$. Finally, 2-point RANSAC rejects outlier feature tracks.

### Inputs

* **Flow-Propagated Events:** $\tilde{l}_k^i = x_k + (T_{i+1} - t_k)u_i$ from Step 1.
* **Initial Onset Template:** $\tilde{l}_j^{i^*}$ from time $T_{i^*}$ when the feature was first initialized.
* **Relative 3D Rotation:** ${}^{i^*}R_i$ between current camera frame $i$ and onset camera frame $i^*$ (computed from the EKF state quaternion estimates).
* **Estimated Optical Flow:** $u_i$ from Step 1.

### Outputs

* **Refined Inlier Feature Positions:** $f(T_{i+1})$ at time $T_{i+1}$.
* **Completed/Lost Feature Tracks:** Tracks that failed RANSAC or exited the field of view, queued for MSCKF marginalization.

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Rotate Propagated Events to Initial Camera Frame $i^*$

Using perspective projection $\pi\left(\begin{bmatrix} X & Y & Z \end{bmatrix}^T\right) = \frac{1}{Z}\begin{bmatrix} X \\ Y \end{bmatrix}$:

$$
y_k^i = \pi\left({}^{i^*}R_i \begin{bmatrix} \tilde{l}_k^i \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\right) - \pi\left({}^{i^*}R_i \begin{bmatrix} f(T_i) + u_i dt_i \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\right)
$$

#### 2. Iterate EM Step 2 Until Convergence $(\text{cost} \le \epsilon_2)$

**E-Step:**

$$
r_{kj} = \frac{\phi(y_k^i; \tilde{l}_j^{i^*}, \Sigma)}{\sum_{j'=1}^{n_{i^*}} \phi(y_k^i; \tilde{l}_{j'}^{i^*}, \Sigma)}
$$

**M-Step (Scaled ICP Update):**

First compute the centroids:

$$
\bar{y} = \frac{1}{n_i} \sum_{k=1}^{n_i} y_k^i, \quad \bar{l} = \frac{1}{n_{i^*}} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i^*}} \tilde{l}_j^{i^*}
$$

Next compute the scale $\sigma$ and translation drift $b$:

$$
\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i^*}} r_{kj} (y_k^i - \bar{y})^T (\tilde{l}_j^{i^*} - \bar{l})}{\sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i^*}} r_{kj} (y_k^i - \bar{y})^T (y_k^i - \bar{y})}}
$$

$$
b = \frac{\sigma}{n_i} \sum_{k=1}^{n_i} y_k^i - \frac{1}{n_{i^*}} \sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i^*}} r_{kj} \tilde{l}_j^{i^*}
$$

**Cost Function Check:**

$$
\text{cost} = \sum_{k=1}^{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_{i^*}} r_{kj} \parallel \sigma y_k^i - b - \tilde{l}_j^{i^*} \parallel^2
$$

#### 3. Update Feature Position

$$
f(T_{i+1}) = f(T_i) - b + dt_i u_i
$$

#### 4. Two-Point RANSAC Outlier Rejection

Given correspondences $(x_1, x_2)$ across consecutive frames and known rotation $R$ from the filter, hypothesize translation direction $t$ from 2 correspondences, construct the Essential Matrix $E = \lfloor t \times \rfloor R$, and classify inliers via Sampson error:

$$
e = \frac{x_2^T E x_1}{(Ex_1)_1^2 + (Ex_1)_2^2 + (E^T x_2)_1^2 + (E^T x_2)_2^2}
$$

## Step 3: Adaptive Window Sizing & New Feature Detection

### General Overview

To maintain the constant optical flow assumption without suffering from severe motion blur or sparse event starvation, the temporal window size $dt_{i+1}$ is dynamically scaled based on the median optical flow speed so that features move approximately $k = 3$ pixels per window. If the number of tracked features drops below a threshold, new corners are initialized by projecting the current window's events onto a 2D edge image.

### Inputs

* **Optical Flow Vectors:** $\{u_i\}$ for all currently active inlier features $f \in \mathcal{F}$.
* **Current Window Events:** $W_i$.

### Outputs

* **Next Temporal Window Duration:** $dt_{i+1}$.
* **Newly Initialized Features:** Added to active feature set $\mathcal{F}$.

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Compute Next Temporal Window Size $dt_{i+1}$

For a desired displacement of $k = 3$ pixels:

$$
dt(f) = \frac{k}{\parallel u_i \parallel_2}
$$

$$
dt_{i+1} = \text{median}\left(\left\{ \frac{3}{\parallel u_i \parallel_2} \;\middle\mid\; f \in \mathcal{F} \right\}\right)
$$

*Note: At system startup $t=0$, initialize $dt_0$ as the time required to accumulate $50,000$ raw events.*

#### 2. New Feature Detection (when $\lvert\mathcal{F}\rvert < N_{\text{thresh}}$)

* Project all events in $[T_i, T_{i+1}]$ orthogonally onto the 2D image plane.
* Divide the image into a fixed grid of cells.
* Run **FAST** corner detection in cells lacking active features and select the corner with the highest **Shi-Tomasi** score per cell.

## Step 4: IMU State & Covariance Propagation (Backend - MSCKF)

### General Overview

As IMU measurements $(a_m, \omega_m)$ arrive between visual update windows $[T_i, T_{i+1}]$, the evolving IMU state estimate $\hat{X}_{IMU}$ is propagated forward in time using 5th-order Runge-Kutta numerical integration, and the state covariance matrix $P$ is propagated via the linearized continuous-time state transition dynamics.

### Inputs

* **Prior State & Covariance:** $\hat{X}_{k\mid k}$ and $P_{k\mid k}$.
* **IMU Measurements:** Rotational velocity $\omega_m$ and linear acceleration $a_m$ over sampling period $T = \tau_{k+1} - \tau_k$.
* **Continuous-Time IMU Noise Covariance:** $Q_{IMU} = \text{diag}(\sigma_g^2 I_3, \sigma_{wg}^2 I_3, \sigma_a^2 I_3, \sigma_{wa}^2 I_3)$.

### Outputs

* **Propagated IMU State:** $\hat{X}_{IMU_{k+1\mid k}}$.
* **Propagated Full Covariance:** $P_{k+1\mid k}$.

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Bias-Corrected IMU Signals

$$
\hat{\omega} = \omega_m - \hat{b}_g - C_{\hat{q}}\omega_G, \quad \hat{a} = a_m - \hat{b}_a
$$

*Note: For small-scale indoor environments as in Zhu et al., Earth rotation $\omega_G$ can be set to $0_{3\times 1}$. For large-scale outdoor ECEF navigation as in Mourikis & Roumeliotis, include $\omega_G$. Here $C_{\hat{q}} = C({}^I_G\hat{\bar{q}})$ denotes the rotation matrix.*

#### 2. Continuous-Time IMU State Differential Equations (Integrate via 5th-Order Runge-Kutta)

$$
{}^I_G\dot{\hat{\bar{q}}} = \frac{1}{2}\Omega(\hat{\omega}){}^I_G\hat{\bar{q}}, \quad \dot{\hat{b}}_g = 0_{3\times 1}
$$

$$
{}^G\dot{\hat{v}}_I = C_{\hat{q}}^T \hat{a} - 2\lfloor \omega_G \times \rfloor {}^G\hat{v}_I - \lfloor \omega_G \times \rfloor^2 {}^G\hat{p}_I + {}^G g
$$

$$
\dot{\hat{b}}_a = 0_{3\times 1}, \quad {}^G\dot{\hat{p}}_I = {}^G\hat{v}_I
$$

Here, the skew-symmetric matrix $\lfloor \omega \times \rfloor$ and quaternion multiplier $\Omega(\omega)$ are:

$$
\lfloor \omega \times \rfloor = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_z & \omega_y \\ \omega_z & 0 & -\omega_x \\ -\omega_y & \omega_x & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \Omega(\omega) = \begin{bmatrix} -\lfloor \omega \times \rfloor & \omega \\ -\omega^T & 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

#### 3. Linearized Error-State Jacobians $F$ and $G$

$$
F = \begin{bmatrix} -\lfloor \hat{\omega} \times \rfloor & -I_3 & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \\ -C_{\hat{q}}^T \lfloor \hat{a} \times \rfloor & 0_{3\times 3} & -2\lfloor \omega_G \times \rfloor & -C_{\hat{q}}^T & -\lfloor \omega_G \times \rfloor^2 \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & I_3 & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
G = \begin{bmatrix} -I_3 & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \\ 0_{3\times 3} & I_3 & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & -C_{\hat{q}}^T & 0_{3\times 3} \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & I_3 \\ 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 3} \end{bmatrix}
$$

#### 4. Propagate State Transition Matrix $\Phi$ and IMU Covariance $P_{II}$

Numerically integrate over $\tau \in [0, T]$ with initial conditions $\Phi(t_k, t_k) = I_{15}$ and $P_{II}(t_k) = P_{II_{k\mid k}}$:

$$
\dot{\Phi}(t_k + \tau, t_k) = F \Phi(t_k + \tau, t_k)
$$

$$
\dot{P}_{II} = F P_{II} + P_{II} F^T + G Q_{IMU} G^T
$$

#### 5. Update Full Partitioned Covariance Matrix

$$
P_{k+1\mid k} = \begin{bmatrix} P_{II_{k+1\mid k}} & \Phi(t_k + T, t_k) P_{IC_{k\mid k}} \\ P_{IC_{k\mid k}}^T \Phi(t_k + T, t_k)^T & P_{CC_{k\mid k}} \end{bmatrix}
$$

## Step 5: State & Covariance Augmentation (Backend - MSCKF)

### General Overview

Whenever a temporal event window $[T_i, T_{i+1}]$ completes in the frontend, a new camera pose corresponding to time $T_{i+1}$ must be appended to the EKF state vector and covariance matrix so that future multi-view constraints can reference it.

### Inputs

* **Propagated State & Covariance:** $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k}$ and $P_{k+1\mid k}$.
* **Known Extrinsic Calibration:** Rotation ${}^C_I\bar{q}$ and translation ${}^I p_C$ from the IMU frame $\{I\}$ to the camera frame $\{C\}$.

### Outputs

* **Augmented State Vector:** $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k}$ with $N+1$ camera poses.
* **Augmented Covariance Matrix:** $P_{k+1\mid k}$ of dimension $((15 + 6(N+1)) \times (15 + 6(N+1)))$.

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Compute New Camera Pose Estimate from Current IMU Pose

$$
{}^C_G\hat{\bar{q}} = {}^C_I\bar{q} \otimes {}^I_G\hat{\bar{q}}, \quad {}^G\hat{p}_C = {}^G\hat{p}_I + C_{\hat{q}}^T {}^I p_C
$$

*Note: If IMU and camera frames are assumed coincident as in the DAVIS-240C formulation of Zhu et al., ${}^C_I\bar{q} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}^T$ and ${}^I p_C = 0_{3\times 1}$.*

#### 2. Construct Augmentation Jacobian $J$ of size $(6 \times (15 + 6N))$

$$
J = \begin{bmatrix} C({}^C_I\bar{q}) & 0_{3\times 9} & 0_{3\times 3} & 0_{3\times 6N} \\ \lfloor C_{\hat{q}}^T {}^I p_C \times \rfloor & 0_{3\times 9} & I_3 & 0_{3\times 6N} \end{bmatrix}
$$

#### 3. Augment the Covariance Matrix

$$
P_{k+1\mid k} \leftarrow \begin{bmatrix} I_{15+6N} \\ J \end{bmatrix} P_{k+1\mid k} \begin{bmatrix} I_{15+6N} \\ J \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

## Step 6: Feature Triangulation & Linearized Measurement Model (Backend - MSCKF)

### General Overview

An EKF update is triggered when either:

1. A tracked feature $f_j$ is lost/completed in the frontend (moves out of view or fails tracking), **or**
2. The number of camera poses reaches $N_{max}$ (in which case $N_{max}/3$ evenly spaced poses starting from the second-oldest pose are selected for removal, and all features observed in those poses are processed).

For each feature $f_j$ to be marginalized, we first run a reprojection RANSAC check to remove outlier observations, triangulate its 3D global position ${}^G\hat{p}_{f_j}$ using Gauss-Newton optimization over inverse depth, and linearize the perspective measurement residuals around the current state and feature estimates.

### Inputs

* **Feature Tracks to Marginalize:** 2D observations $z_i^{(j)}$ for pose indices $i \in \mathcal{S}_j$ (where $M_j = \lvert\mathcal{S}_j\rvert$ is the number of poses that observed feature $f_j$).
* **Camera Pose Estimates:** $\left({}^{C_i}_G\hat{\bar{q}}, {}^G\hat{p}_{C_i}\right)$ for $i \in \mathcal{S}_j$ from the state vector.

### Outputs

* **Stacked Measurement Residual per Feature:** $r^{(j)} \in \mathbb{R}^{2M_j}$.
* **Stacked State & Feature Jacobians per Feature:** $H_X^{(j)} \in \mathbb{R}^{2M_j \times (15+6N)}$ and $H_f^{(j)} \in \mathbb{R}^{2M_j \times 3}$.

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Inverse-Depth Gauss-Newton Triangulation

Let $\{C_n\}$ be the first camera frame where feature $f_j$ was observed. Parameterize its 3D coordinates using inverse depth $\alpha_j = \frac{{}^{C_n}X_j}{{}^{C_n}Z_j}$, $\beta_j = \frac{{}^{C_n}Y_j}{{}^{C_n}Z_j}$, and $\rho_j = \frac{1}{{}^{C_n}Z_j}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} h_{i1}(\alpha_j, \beta_j, \rho_j) \\ h_{i2}(\alpha_j, \beta_j, \rho_j) \\ h_{i3}(\alpha_j, \beta_j, \rho_j) \end{bmatrix} = C({}^{C_i}_{C_n}\hat{\bar{q}}) \begin{bmatrix} \alpha_j \\ \beta_j \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + \rho_j {}^{C_i}\hat{p}_{C_n}
$$

Minimize reprojection error over $i \in \mathcal{S}_j$ via Gauss-Newton to find $\hat{\alpha}_j, \hat{\beta}_j, \hat{\rho}_j$, where the predicted measurement is:

$$
\hat{z}_i^{(j)} = \frac{1}{h_{i3}} \begin{bmatrix} h_{i1} \\ h_{i2} \end{bmatrix}
$$

Convert the converged estimate to global 3D position:

$$
{}^G\hat{p}_{f_j} = \frac{1}{\hat{\rho}_j} C^T({}^{C_n}_G\hat{\bar{q}}) \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\alpha}_j \\ \hat{\beta}_j \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + {}^G\hat{p}_{C_n}
$$

#### 2. Compute Reprojection Residual for Each Observation $i \in \mathcal{S}_j$

Transform the global feature into camera frame $C_i$:

$$
{}^{C_i}\hat{p}_{f_j} = \begin{bmatrix} {}^{C_i}\hat{X}_j \\ {}^{C_i}\hat{Y}_j \\ {}^{C_i}\hat{Z}_j \end{bmatrix} = C({}^{C_i}_G\hat{\bar{q}})\left({}^G\hat{p}_{f_j} - {}^G\hat{p}_{C_i}\right)
$$

$$
r_i^{(j)} = z_i^{(j)} - \hat{z}_i^{(j)} = z_i^{(j)} - \frac{1}{{}^{C_i}\hat{Z}_j} \begin{bmatrix} {}^{C_i}\hat{X}_j \\ {}^{C_i}\hat{Y}_j \end{bmatrix}
$$

#### 3. Compute Measurement Jacobians $H_{X_i}^{(j)}$ and $H_{f_i}^{(j)}$

Define the perspective projection Jacobian $J_i$:

$$
J_i = \frac{1}{{}^{C_i}\hat{Z}_j} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & -\frac{{}^{C_i}\hat{X}_j}{{}^{C_i}\hat{Z}_j} \\ 0 & 1 & -\frac{{}^{C_i}\hat{Y}_j}{{}^{C_i}\hat{Z}_j} \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Jacobian w.r.t. Feature Position $(2 \times 3)$:**

$$
H_{f_i}^{(j)} = J_i C({}^{C_i}_G\hat{\bar{q}})
$$

**Jacobian w.r.t. State Vector $(2 \times (15 + 6N))$:**

Non-zero only at the 6 columns corresponding to camera pose $C_i$:

$$
H_{X_i}^{(j)} = \begin{bmatrix} 0_{2\times 15} & \cdots & J_i \lfloor {}^{C_i}\hat{p}_{f_j} \times \rfloor & -J_i C({}^{C_i}_G\hat{\bar{q}}) & \cdots \end{bmatrix}
$$

#### 4. Stack Across All $M_j$ Observations of Feature $f_j$

$$
r^{(j)} \simeq H_X^{(j)}\tilde{X} + H_f^{(j)}{}^G\tilde{p}_{f_j} + n^{(j)}
$$

with covariance $R^{(j)} = \sigma_{im}^2 I_{2M_j}$.

## Step 7: Nullspace Marginalization, QR Reduction & EKF Update (Backend - MSCKF)

### General Overview

Because ${}^G\hat{p}_{f_j}$ was estimated using the camera poses in $\hat{X}$, the feature error ${}^G\tilde{p}_{f_j}$ is correlated with the state error $\tilde{X}$. To apply a valid EKF update without putting features in the state vector, $r^{(j)}$ is projected onto the left nullspace of $H_f^{(j)}$, completely eliminating ${}^G\tilde{p}_{f_j}$. All uncorrelated feature residuals are then stacked, compressed via QR decomposition to reduce computational complexity, and used to update the state and covariance.

### Inputs

* **Stacked Residuals & Jacobians:** $r^{(j)}, H_X^{(j)}, H_f^{(j)}$ for all $L$ marginalized features $(j = 1, \dots, L)$.
* **Prior State & Covariance:** $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k}$ and $P_{k+1\mid k}$.
* **Image Pixel Noise Variance:** $\sigma_{im}^2$.

### Outputs

* **Posterior Updated State:** $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k+1}$.
* **Posterior Updated Covariance:** $P_{k+1\mid k+1}$ (with pruned camera states removed if $N_{max}$ was reached).

### Equations to Implement

#### 1. Left Nullspace Projection (Structureless Marginalization)

For each feature $j$, let $A_j$ be the $2M_j \times (2M_j - 3)$ unitary matrix whose columns form the basis of the left nullspace of $H_f^{(j)}$ (i.e., $A_j^T H_f^{(j)} = 0$, computed via Givens rotations or QR decomposition of $H_f^{(j)}$):

$$
r_o^{(j)} = A_j^T r^{(j)} \simeq A_j^T H_X^{(j)} \tilde{X} + A_j^T n^{(j)} = H_o^{(j)} \tilde{X} + n_o^{(j)}
$$

Here, $r_o^{(j)}$ has dimension $(2M_j - 3) \times 1$ and noise covariance $R_o^{(j)} = \sigma_{im}^2 I_{2M_j - 3}$.

*Note: Before stacking, perform a Mahalanobis gating test on $r_o^{(j)}$ against $\chi^2$ thresholds to reject moving objects and outliers.*

#### 2. Stack Residuals Across All $L$ Features

Let $d = \sum_{j=1}^L (2M_j - 3)$. Stack $r_o^{(j)}$ and $H_o^{(j)}$ into a single system:

$$
r_o = H_X \tilde{X} + n_o, \quad R_o = \sigma_{im}^2 I_d
$$

#### 3. QR Decomposition for Dimensionality Reduction

When $d > 15 + 6N$, decompose $H_X$ using QR decomposition (where $T_H$ is an $r \times (15+6N)$ upper triangular matrix and $Q_1$ has $r$ columns):

$$
H_X = \begin{bmatrix} Q_1 & Q_2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} T_H \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Project $r_o$ onto the range space $Q_1$:

$$
r_n = Q_1^T r_o = T_H \tilde{X} + n_n, \quad R_n = \sigma_{im}^2 I_r
$$

#### 4. Compute Kalman Gain & State Correction

$$
K = P_{k+1\mid k} T_H^T \left( T_H P_{k+1\mid k} T_H^T + R_n \right)^{-1}
$$

$$
\Delta X = K r_n
$$

Add $\Delta X$ to the state vector $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k}$ (using quaternion multiplication $\hat{\bar{q}} \leftarrow \delta\bar{q}(\Delta\theta) \otimes \hat{\bar{q}}$ for orientations and standard addition for vectors).

#### 5. Update State Covariance Matrix (Joseph Form)

Let $\xi = 15 + 6N$:

$$
P_{k+1\mid k+1} = \left(I_\xi - K T_H\right) P_{k+1\mid k} \left(I_\xi - K T_H\right)^T + K R_n K^T
$$

*Note: If the update was triggered by reaching $N_{max}$ poses, drop the rows and columns of $P_{k+1\mid k+1}$ and elements of $\hat{X}_{k+1\mid k+1}$ corresponding to the marginalized camera poses.*
